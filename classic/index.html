Mascot GeneratorPrototype

Mascot Generator is an open-source tool in the making. You build a mascot from ready-made parts and keep it in a library. AI agents then pick it up through MCP and use it in videos, games and apps.

Exports glTF (.glb) with a skeleton and named moves. Color-flow and cut-out finishes export as vinyl.
